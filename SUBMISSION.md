## Timing of the hardening flow (limit: 6 h for gds + gl_test together)

Runs #65 and #79 used the Tiny Tapeout SG13G2 flow at 6x4 tiles. The runs after that use the SG13 CMOS5L flow at 8x4 tiles, which is slower.

| Run | gds | gl_test | Whole workflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 (6 Oct 2026) | 2 h 40 min | 3 h 11 min | 5 h 52 min |
| #79 (11 Oct 2026, SG13G2 flow, 6x4, QSPI divider set first in the gate-level images) | 2 h 35 min | 11 min | 2 h 46 min |
| #85/#88 (11-12 Oct 2026, CMOS5L flow, 8x4, density 60 %) | 5 h 47 min / 3 h 49 min | 10 min | precheck 4 h 28 min / 4 h 48 min; precheck failed with one M2.b violation |
| #90 (12-13 Oct 2026, CMOS5L flow, 8x4, density 58 %) | 4 h 34 min | 8 min | 9 h 22 min; precheck 4 h 47 min, passed 9/9 |

The gds job on CMOS5L ranges from 3 h 49 min to 5 h 47 min between runs, so gds plus gl_test leaves a buffer of only about 1 to 2 h under the six-hour limit. Two other layout variants (density 55 %, clock 41 ns) hit the six-hour limit in gds. The precheck is a separate job; its KLayout DRC takes about 4.8 h on the 8x4 die.

## Tile size and the CMOS5L flow

- The design is hardened at **8x4 tiles**. At 6x4 detailed routing did not converge (congestion), so 6x4 is not met. The brief gives 6x4 as the current area limit and 8x4 as the planned maximum; the organisers' 8x4 template is expected in mid-October. **Ask the organisers** which size applies.
- `src/config.json` uses `PL_TARGET_DENSITY_PCT` 58. At 60 the precheck reported one Metal2 spacing violation (rule M2.b, 0.21 um, about 1 nm short at one corner near x 65.9 um, y 456.7 um); 58 gives a clean precheck. The flow is deterministic, so the same inputs give the same layout.
- The gate-level Makefile reads `<lib>_udp.v` before `<lib>_stdcell.v`; the CMOS5L cell library keeps its UDP primitives in a separate file.

## Start-up state (reset)

The register file, the RAM, the PIO instruction memory and the PIO FIFO memory have no reset on silicon. The tests start
them with random contents: `make RANDOM_INIT=<seed>` (cocotb, in `test/`) and `make benches RANDOM_INIT=<seed>` (Icarus
benches); the list of registers is generated by `tools/gen_random_init.py`. The seeds run in CI are listed in
`.github/workflows/pio-tests.yaml`. A program must still write a register, RAM byte or PIO word before reading it.

## Known limits (stated honestly)

- Not tested on silicon or an FPGA board for the C and 7-segment examples; they ran on the RTL compiled to WebAssembly.
- Formal verification covers `pio_fifo.v` and `pio_sm.v` only (`formal/`, unbounded proofs; clock-divider timing for dividers below 8.0). `pio.v`, the CPU core, `mem.v` and the QSPI engine are not formally verified. Constrained-random tests (`test/test_pio_random.py`) cover UART, SPI, I2C and PS/2 only; CAN, 1-Wire, JTAG, SWD, USB and WS2812 use directed tests and the mutation sweeps.
- Hardened at 8x4 only; 6x4 did not route. The Metal2 corner violation at density 60 % shows the layout is sensitive to small changes; re-run the flow after any change.
- Pads of the CMOS5L process are not characterised yet (organisers' statement); external QSPI flash / PSRAM timing is untested on hardware.
- Only the smoke and signature PIO tests run on the placed (gate-level) netlist.
- A design that is deselected loses its state; the program has to be loaded again.
